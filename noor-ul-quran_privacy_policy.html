Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 19, 2026

Welcome to Noor-ul-Quran ("we," "our," or "the app"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application handles information when you use it.

By downloading and using Noor-ul-Quran, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. About Noor-ul-Quran

Noor-ul-Quran is a free Quran reader and audio player. It lets you read the Holy Quran in Arabic alongside translations, listen to recitations from renowned reciters, and personalize your reading experience with selectable Arabic fonts, font sizes, and reader themes.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

Noor-ul-Quran is designed with privacy in mind. We do not collect, store, or transmit any of the following:

  • Personally identifiable information (name, email, phone number)
  • Account credentials — the app does not require you to create an account
  • Location data
  • Contacts, messages, photos, or files
  • Reading history, bookmarks, or personal notes are stored only on your device and never sent anywhere

3. Information Stored on Your Device

The following data is stored locally on your device to provide and remember your preferences. It never leaves your device:

  • Selected app language and theme
  • Arabic font choice and font size preferences
  • Selected reciter and playback speed
  • Downloaded translation files

You can clear this data at any time by uninstalling the app or clearing app storage from your device settings.

4. Permissions Used

  • Internet access: Required to stream recitation audio and to download translations. No personal data is sent over this connection.
  • Notifications (optional): Used to send a daily reading reminder, if you enable it. You can disable notifications at any time from your device settings.

5. Third-Party Services

Recitation audio and translation text are fetched from public Quran APIs. These services may log standard technical request data (such as IP address) according to their own privacy policies, but Noor-ul-Quran itself does not share any of your personal information with them.

The app does not include advertising SDKs, analytics trackers, or third-party login services.

6. Children's Privacy

Noor-ul-Quran is suitable for users of all ages and is safe for children. We do not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

7. Data Security

Because Noor-ul-Quran does not collect or transmit personal data, there is no personal data of yours at risk. Locally stored preferences remain on your device under the protection of your device's own security model.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this page periodically.

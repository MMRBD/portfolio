Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

Noor-ul-Quran is a Qur'an reader with optional recitation, translation downloads, reminders, and Qibla tools. This policy explains the data used by the Android app.

Data stored on your device

The app stores your bookmarks, last reading position, search history, language, theme, reader settings, reminder settings, and selected reciter in local preferences. The Qur'an database, downloaded translations, and recitation audio are also stored on your device. You do not need an account.

If Android backup or device transfer is enabled on your device, Android may back up or transfer local preferences, including bookmarks and last reading position. The app excludes downloaded audio and its Qur'an database from these backups. You can manage backup in your device settings. Clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it removes locally stored app data, subject to any backups managed by Android.

Location

With your permission, the Qibla compass reads your device's location while that screen is in use to calculate the direction to Mecca. The app does not save that location or send its coordinates to our servers. The Nearby Mosque tool can open a separate maps app, which handles data under its own privacy policy. You can deny or revoke location permission in Android settings.

Internet and third parties

The app uses public Qur'an services to fetch recitation audio, reciter information, and translation editions, and a public service for the Names of Allah. Those services receive ordinary network request information, such as your IP address and the content requested, under their own policies. Reading the Qur'an text already included in the app works offline. The app has no ads and does not require a sign-in.

Crash and performance diagnostics

The app sends crash reports and sampled performance diagnostics to Sentry to help us find and fix problems. Reports can include app and device details, operating system version, stack traces, interaction breadcrumbs, network request details, and technical information such as an IP address processed by the service. We do not use this data for advertising. Sentry processes it on our behalf under its own privacy terms. Diagnostic retention is controlled by our Sentry project settings.

Notifications

With notification permission, the app can show optional daily reminders and progress or controls for audio playback and downloads. You can disable notifications in Android settings and change reminders in the app.

Your choices and contact

You can manage permissions and backup in Android settings, disable reminders in the app, and remove locally stored app data by clearing storage or uninstalling. For questions about this policy or diagnostic data, email mmrrahman508@gmail.com.
